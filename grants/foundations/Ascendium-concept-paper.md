**Working title:** Computing coursework infrastructure that works where the internet doesn't: community colleges, rural campuses, and (eventually) corrections education

**Request:** $650,000 over 36 months

## The gap

Hands-on computing and data coursework increasingly assumes infrastructure that under-resourced institutions don't have: commercial cloud platforms, reliable broadband, IT staff to integrate vendor tools, and data-sharing agreements that small colleges can't negotiate. The August 2026 retirement of GitHub Classroom — the free tool many community colleges quietly depended on — turns a chronic gap into an acute one. The colleges serving the learners Ascendium prioritizes are precisely the ones most likely to respond by abandoning industry-standard workflows altogether, which transfers the cost onto students as weaker preparation for transfer and employment.

## The intervention

EduCloud is open-source course infrastructure (assignment distribution, collection, sandboxed autograding, immediate feedback) designed around the constraints of low-resource settings rather than against them: **self-hostable by one operator on commodity hardware; functional offline or on intermittent connectivity** (a deliberate design commitment — mastery-based bridge curriculum included); **privacy by architecture** (stores Git usernames, never student records — no data-sharing agreement required); free forever to self-host, with a managed tier whose onboarding is philanthropy-subsidized for institutions without IT capacity (the grant-to-fee model CUNY's Transfer Explorer demonstrated in 2026). Fall 2026 pilots ran across a community-college → university transfer pathway and four-year adopters. **[PILOT DATA.]**

## What Ascendium funding does

1. **Community-college capacity (core):** subsidized onboarding, instructor training (Carpentries-style), and an openly licensed mastery-based data-literacy/Python bridge for **eight community colleges**, weighted toward rural-serving institutions.
2. **Rural/low-connectivity hardening:** ship and document the fully offline deployment profile (local-network operation, sync-on-connect), tested at partner rural campuses.
3. **Corrections-education feasibility (exploratory, ~15% of budget):** a structured feasibility study with one or two higher-education-in-prison programs. EduCloud's architecture happens to match the central technical constraints of that setting — air-gapped operation, no student PII, no surveillance features (an explicit, documented refusal), auditable open source — but we will not claim product-market fit we haven't earned; this phase produces requirements, partnerships, and a go/no-go, not a deployment.
4. **Evidence:** external evaluation of learner outcomes and institutional adoption costs, published openly.

## Outcomes (36 months)

Eight community colleges in sustained production (≥2,000 learners); a documented offline deployment in real rural use; published cost-per-institution and cost-per-learner figures and a replication playbook; a corrections-education requirements report with named program partners and a candid go/no-go; transfer-pathway outcome data from the pathway sites.

## Budget sketch

CC onboarding subsidy + instructor training ~$200K; offline/rural engineering and field testing ~$120K; corrections feasibility (partner stipends, site work, report) ~$95K; external evaluation ~$85K; program lead (part-time, 3 yrs) ~$115K; travel/convening ~$35K.
