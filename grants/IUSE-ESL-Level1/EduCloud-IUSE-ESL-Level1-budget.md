**F. Participant Support — $3,000.** Stipends/refreshments for instructor professional development and a dissemination convening. (IUSE does not fund student scholarships; this line is participant — not student-aid — costs, excluded from the F&A base.)

Other Direct Costs.** *External evaluator (consultant), $12,000/yr* — `[CRED (UArizona) · external TBD (Willamette)]` — formative and summative evaluation with dedicated effort, as IUSE requires. *Graduate tuition remission, $12,000* — F&A-excluded. *Materials, modest survey incentives, software, publication/open-access, $2,500.*
