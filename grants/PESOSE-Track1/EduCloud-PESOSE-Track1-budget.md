# PESOSE Track 1 — Budget & Justification (Working Draft)

**Solicitation:** NSF 26-506, Track 1 (Scoping & Planning)
**Period:** 1 year · **Total must not exceed $300,000** (inclusive of F&A)
**Cost sharing:** prohibited · **F&A:** institution's negotiated rate, no program cap
**Status:** planning estimates — confirm salaries, fringe, and F&A with Sponsored Projects, and check the PESOSE website for I-Corps budget specifics

> A **1-year planning budget**, deliberately distinct from the multi-year operating model. It funds scoping, governance, security/privacy planning, ecosystem discovery, and community formation — **not product development** (which Track 1 does not support). It is structured around the lean proposing team: PI + co-PI (Cruze) + senior personnel (Markwart) + a graduate assistant.

## Justification

**A. Senior Personnel.**
- *PI `[name]`, $38,000* — approximately one summer month plus partial academic-year effort leading ecosystem discovery, governance and licensing design, the security/privacy planning, and the mandatory I-Corps for PESOSE activities. Consistent with Track 1, PI effort is scoping and ecosystem formation, not product development.
- *Co-PI A. Cruze, $12,000* — leads the **Organization & Governance** deliverable, the sustainability plan, and I-Corps participation, drawing on nonprofit, grant-writing, and program-development experience. `[Affiliation pending — if external to the submitting institution, restructure as a subaward or consultant under G; totals shift modestly.]`

**B. Other Personnel.**
- *B. Markwart — Research Software Engineer / Technical Writer (0.25 FTE), $26,000* — authors the **planning deliverables**: the documented threat model and security/privacy plan, SBOM and signed-release design, governance and contribution-process documentation, and the **registry/federation interoperability design** (specification and ecosystem-mapping — design and documentation, not feature development, per Track 1 rules).
- *Graduate Research Assistant (0.5 FTE), $24,000* — conducts the ecosystem-discovery interviews and educator survey, the landscape analysis, and community-metrics design. Tuition remission, if required, is an additional F&A-excluded cost; trim senior effort to stay within the cap.

**C. Fringe Benefits — $25,680.** Institution's negotiated rates (`[sr. ~30%; GRA ~12%]` placeholders) — confirm with Sponsored Projects.

**D. Equipment — $0.** Compute for scoping pilots is met through national cyberinfrastructure allocations (ACCESS/CyVerse), not purchase.

**E. Travel — $20,000.** (1) Mandatory **I-Corps for PESOSE** cohort participation (confirm expectations and any prescribed budget on the PESOSE website); (2) dissemination and community-discovery travel to SIGCSE and PEARC.

**F. Participant Support Costs — $25,000.** Stipends and travel for **external** participants — early adopters and prospective contributors who are not project personnel — to a community-building workshop/sprint. Directly serves the Community Building activity and seeds the external contributor base a future Track 2 proposal requires. Excluded from the F&A base.

**G. Other Direct Costs — $27,000.** (1) **External evaluator** (consultant), $15,000 — formative and summative assessment of scoping deliverables and milestones; (2) **legal, trademark, and fiscal-sponsor onboarding**, $8,000; (3) **materials, software, and dissemination**, $4,000.

**H. Total Direct Costs — $197,680.**

**I. Indirect Costs (F&A) — $94,974.** Negotiated rate (`[55% MTDC]` placeholder for UArizona) on modified total direct costs (direct less participant support). PESOSE imposes no F&A cap.

**J. Total — $292,654**, within the $300,000 Track 1 limit.
