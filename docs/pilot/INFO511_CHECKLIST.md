- [ ] **6. Accept teaching and accessibility.** Greg and independent testers. Complete fresh no-AI and permitted-help journeys, review representative hints with the published rubric, and exercise keyboard, screen reader, zoom, expiry and error recovery from login through receipt. Check that a tester can explain their result independently. Distinguish an ordinary browser close from a failed workflow in the instructor launcher and retain the original failure.
- [ ] **7. Decide student admission.** Greg and service owner. Rehearse the support contact, backup operator, stop procedure and instructor fallback. Review results against the exact release configuration, record go/no-go and reconcile the umbrella evidence pointers. Track time to submission, failed attempts and support minutes using approved data. Broker-managed compute additionally requires [Outfitter placement and cleanup qualification](https://github.com/EduCloud-Ecosystem/outfitter/issues/5).
- [ ] **After initial usability feedback, decide which optional features to integrate.** Waypoint and instructor. Record adopt, revise or defer for cues/tour, diagram and lecture navigation. For each adopted change, verify the packaged version on the host, accessible operation, tutor boundaries and the ordinary saved-file submission workflow.

At each handoff, update the relevant item with its date, owner, evidence link or immutable revision, and remaining failures. Check it off when its stated outcome has been observed. Keep private run output local and link only sanitized evidence from the public repo.
