## Cohesive fix and preserved authority

After whole-path diagnosis, the final fix removes repeated pure metadata work:
exact JSON comparison and copy-on-write candidate projection are local to one
immutable transition classification. The already-validated v7 Store payload
uses the same integer/depth token loop without repeating its full shape pass.
Independent legacy oracles cover comparison masks, lexical numbers, depth,
public closed decoders, transition/Advance behavior and Store-read equivalence.
No cross-call authority cache, batching or new ledger is introduced.

The merged increment also contains reviewed narrow already-denied negative Stop
rescue, checked cleanup acknowledgement and successful-only final result
observation controls. An already committed absorbing denial can avoid its
semantic no-op mutation; an initially non-denied invocation still requires its
own definitive denial acknowledgement. Any ambiguous first publication skips
all provider calls in that invocation, without readback fallback. A prior
acknowledged stopped observation can avoid only the redundant same-invocation
no-op. Later guards still read current state. Successful final observation is a
pure result projection, never authority for a later cleanup/provider action.

All required current reads, semantic acknowledgements, write/fault opportunities,
clock/event/counter checks, exact identity/generation/provider brackets, original
opaque ownership, sticky close uncertainty and conservative accounting remain.
No physical action, deadline, original four-second assertion, default, public
wire or schema was broadened. The fresh v7 profile and lifecycle helper remain
test-only. Production content/prepare/ingest/seal/execute routes remain disabled
before input or effects; no public flag or imported receipt enables them.

Lost claim acknowledgement or readback never authorizes replay. EOF, process
Wait, PID, a free lease or full charge is not close proof. Committed physical
release after lost final acknowledgement leaves the original operation held;
a distinct claim needs fresh gates and its own acknowledgement. Temporary raw
input/output/stderr/paths stay outside retained Worker, recovery and study exports.
