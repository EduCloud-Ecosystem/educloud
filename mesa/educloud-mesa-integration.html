EduCloud × MESA
the teaching & workforce-development layer for MESA's Open Science cyberinfrastructure
Independent open-source delivers MESA training reuses AI Sandboxes usernames, not PII (FERPA)
EduCloud is an independent, self-hostable teaching platform that becomes the graded, autograded assignment layer for MESA's training program — running on MESA's agentic-AI and data cyberinfrastructure (AI Sandboxes, AI-Verde inference, CyVerse/Jetstream2, iRODS, CI-Logon) and advancing its education, workforce, and broadening-participation goals. It plugs into MESA; MESA does not own it.

EduCloud — the project

independent OSS · own IP & governance

Distributes assignments to per-student Git repos, autogrades them in sandboxed containers, and optionally runs the Sol tutor. Git-host-agnostic, privacy-minimal. AGPL control plane + Apache-2.0 primitives. Home-able at a partner institution. A parallel Registry & federation layer (object catalog + Coolify-backed deployment + Keycloak identity) is in design — federating existing open-source infrastructure rather than rebuilding it.

Data Lakehouse

DuckLake · DuckDB · PostgreSQL
time-travel + ACIDschema-on-read

The first open-source data lakehouse for large NSF programs. DuckLake stores table metadata in SQL (PostgreSQL) with data files in Parquet on S3-compatible storage; Apache Iceberg / Delta compatible. Schema-on-read ingestion, ACID transactions, and time-travel versioning for reproducible science.

What EduCloud delivers to MESA — the EOT / workforce pillar (§3.2.1)

MESA's training is self-paced notebooks + Carpentries workshops today; EduCloud adds the graded, autograded, version-controlled assignment layer
Training delivery (Tier 1–3)

Distributes and autogrades the Foundations, Applied Skills, and Educator-Fellow curricula as real assignments — "dataset → Lakehouse table," metadata harmonization, anomaly detection.feeds EOT 1–6

Assessment backbone

Pre/post competency and autograded checks give the Kirkpatrick Learning and Behavior levels hard data, not just satisfaction surveys.≥25% skill gain · ≥50% reuse

Workforce + participation

Version-controlled, contributable modules let Educator Fellows teach MESA methods at home institutions and broaden participation.~500 trained · ~10 institutions · micro-credentials

Use-case + reusable pattern

A CISE/education use-case and reproducible teaching testbed on MESA — plus a reusable autograding-sandbox pattern for the community.broader impacts · Goal 3

Why it fits IDSS: EduCloud advances MESA's Goal 3 (agentic AI) as a concrete education use-case, carries the §3.2.1 EOT / workforce-development mission, and strengthens broader impacts — while its privacy-by-architecture reinforces MESA's FERPA posture. It is not a single-application tool MESA funds; it is a capability on the CI and a delivery layer for the training MESA must run anyway. Independence holds — both are permissive/AGPL+Apache open source; EduCloud partners with and runs on MESA, so it can also be home-based at a partner institution.
